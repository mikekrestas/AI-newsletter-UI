Your day, in a few lines.

The hosted app works in Safari or Chrome on your phone. AI runs on Cloudflare, so your PC can be switched off. No local model download is needed.

Generate a report

  1. Connect your Gmail account.
  2. Open Daily brief and choose a date.
  3. Check the provider panel: it lists every newsletter with labelled emails received on the chosen date. Tap Generate daily brief. Signal reads all full editions, including read/dismissed emails and sources hidden by Catch up filters. The progress bar shows Collecting, Evaluating, Reporting and Complete using live server stages.
  4. Open Source editions below the report if you want more detail.

Dates use London time even when you travel. Generating a brief does not mark any email read. A very short digest prioritises the main developments; it cannot retain every article's detail.

How stories are selected

Each newsletter provider gets the same maximum candidate budget, so longer emails and frequent senders do not get extra weight. The editor compares all providers for concrete new developments, practical impact and research evidence. Shared stories appear once; adverts are excluded. A draft citing only one provider gets another editorial review of the others.

Source editions lists every email processed, not just the links chosen for highlights. Opening a saved report checks for later arrivals without using AI and flags missing editions. If only one edition is found, check the other newsletters’ Gmail label and arrival date. The brief uses emails received on that London-calendar day; it is not a web-news search.

Read it on another device

Finished reports are saved privately for 30 days. Connect the same Gmail account on your other device and open Daily brief. The saved report appears automatically. A new report can take about a minute to become visible elsewhere. Generate again to include later arrivals; unchanged emails reuse the saved report.

Free, with a daily allowance

This app uses Cloudflare's free Workers AI allowance. If that allowance is exhausted, generation stops with an error. There is no paid fallback. Keep your Cloudflare account on the Free plan.

Where your data goes

Generation sends your newsletter text to Cloudflare AI. Signal stores completed summaries and source metadata without raw email bodies. Persistent Gmail credentials are stored separately and encrypted, and the browser holds an HttpOnly cookie. Reports are restricted to your configured Gmail account. Read, saved and dismissed edition progress stays in each browser.

If you're viewing localhost

The local reader preview cannot run hosted AI. Follow README.md in the downloaded project: run npm install and npm run setup:hosted, then register the resulting HTTPS website origin in your Google OAuth client. Open that website on your phone.
